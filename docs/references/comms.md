# Comms: references

**Compared with:** stalwartlabs/stalwart `23b69755cb3c`, livekit-examples/meet `aed896b0fecd`, zulip/zulip `c803cca1ceac` against ferrum-app-comms 0.7.5 (working tree, `0b9c7d707d3b`). **Date:** 2026-11-01

## What Ferrum has

- Its own React interface at `/mail/`, `/chat/`, `/calendar/`, `/meet/` (`ui/src/main.tsx`). `app.yaml` adds launcher entries, Create > Email, intents (compose email, schedule event, share record), a Chat side panel, bar search, My-work invitations, six notification kinds and a public guest page.
- Webmail on each person's hosted Stalwart mailbox through a JMAP proxy (`src/webmail.rs`): folders, search, sandboxed reader, plain-text compose with Drive attachments, reply and forward, spam training, file to a record, vacation reply, app passwords, shared-mailbox switcher (`ui/src/Mail.tsx`). Mailboxes, shared mailboxes and lists follow the user directory (`src/hosted.rs`, `src/groups.rs`).
- Mail on records: SMTP, IMAP and webhook accounts, threaded `Communication`s, templates, retry, newsletters with signed one-click unsubscribe (`packages/comms/entities/mail.yaml`, `src/mail.rs`, `src/inbound.rs`, `src/newsletter.rs`).
- Chat: public, private and direct channels, one-level threads, edit and delete, read markers, files, search, mention notifications, live push (`ui/src/Chat.tsx`, `src/chat.rs`, `packages/comms/entities/chat.yaml`).
- Calendar: month and week grids, drag to move, recurrence with exceptions, RSVP, ICS invitations, reminders, free/busy endpoint, ICS feed and import, CalDAV and CardDAV (`ui/src/Calendar.tsx`, `src/calendar.rs`, `src/dav.rs`). Meet: LiveKit rooms for channels, events and "meet now", guest links, pop-out window (`ui/src/Meet.tsx`, `src/meet.rs`).
- Gaps already on record: captions, transcripts, recording, presence (FERRUM-COMMS-002 in `ferrum/docs/registry/apps.yaml`); recording, end-to-end encryption and removing someone from a call are "not built" (`ferrum/docs/plan/collaboration.md`, C4).

## What the references do that Ferrum does not

Reference paths are relative to each repo under `referance/comms/` (Meet is `livekit-meet`); Ferrum paths to `ferrum-app-comms/` unless they start with `ferrum/`. Sizes are for one engineer: S days, M one to three weeks, L longer. Stalwart's web admin is not in the checkout (`crates/services/src/lib.rs` unpacks it at start), so its screens are known only from its README.

## Screens to hand-craft

1. **Channel details and notification levels.** Purpose: let owners run a channel and everyone choose how loud it is; today only a Chat Admin can, in the records form.
   - Layout: a right-hand panel opened from the channel header with three tabs, as in Zulip: General (name, topic, public or private, archive; owners), Personal (notify for every message, mentions only or nothing; mute; leave), Members (searchable list with role chips, an add box using the people picker, remove buttons).
   - Seen in the reference: the same three tabs (`stream_edit_toggler.ts`); members edited with pills and a search box (`stream_edit_subscribers.ts`); the Personal tab holds mute, colour and push defaults (`web/templates/stream_settings/stream_settings.hbs`).
   - Proposed: name and topic save on blur; add and remove show at once with an undo toast; leaving as the last owner hands ownership to the next member (the server already does, `leave`); muted channels sort lower and badge only on a mention; the header's member count opens Members.
   - Data: has `PATCH /channels/{id}`, `POST .../members`, `POST .../leave` and the member list (`src/chat.rs`). Lacks a per-member notify level (a select on `ChatChannelMember`), a role change call (none today), and notification kinds for DMs and replies.
2. **Mail domain health.** Purpose: tell the operator exactly which DNS records to publish and whether they are live; hosted mail without them lands in spam.
   - Layout: under Communication Settings, managers only. Header: domain, hosted and shared mailbox counts, last sync (`HostedMailbox.synced_at`), one plain sentence such as "5 of 9 checks pass" (no green badge). Table: Record, Name, Expected value with copy, Found now, Status in words (Missing, Differs, OK), one line on why it matters. Buttons: Check again (shows the last check time), Download zone file, Rotate DKIM key.
   - Seen in the reference: Stalwart builds MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, TLS-RPT, MTA-STS, autoconfig and autodiscover names, SRV, TLSA and CAA for a domain and can write them to more than forty DNS provider types (`dns/records.rs`, 47 `DnsServer*` structs in the registry `structs.rs`); the README lists DKIM rotation.
   - Proposed: an "only problems" toggle; checks on demand and nightly, a failure notifies Comms Managers; reverse DNS and port 25 shown as manual checks with instructions; automatic publishing to a DNS provider is a later step that needs the operator's DNS credentials.
   - Data: has the domain (`CommsSettings.mail_domain`) and the admin connection (`Admin` in `src/hosted.rs`). Lacks the expected records (ask Stalwart for the domain's server-set `dnsZoneFile`; request shape not verified), DNS lookups from the server, and a place to keep the last result.
3. **Event editor with Find a time, and the guest RSVP page.** Purpose: schedule across people without leaving Calendar, and let outside guests answer.
   - Layout: a click on an empty slot opens a popover (title, times, guest chips, video toggle, More). More opens two columns: the form (guests from the people picker or outside emails, time zone, repeat, reminders, location, notes) and Find a time, a 30-minute grid for the day with a row per guest, busy blocks grey (private events read "Busy"), working hours shaded, and the next five slots that suit everyone. The guest page is public, opened from the invitation: Yes, Maybe, No and an optional note.
   - Seen in the reference: Stalwart's RSVP page lets a guest reply with a comment (`crates/groupware/src/calendar/itip.rs`), the invitation template marks changed fields with the old value and carries the RSVP block (`calendar-invite.html`), and inbound replies are applied to the event (`scheduling/inbound.rs`).
   - Proposed: dragging the event block on the grid changes its times; a suggested slot fills the form; the organizer sees each answer and note on the event; reuse the small form in `Intents.tsx` for the popover.
   - Data: has `POST /calendar/freebusy` (up to 50 people, merged busy intervals; `src/calendar.rs`), attendee responses and a time zone per event (`calendar.yaml`). Lacks working hours, a signed RSVP link with a public route (`src/site.rs`), an emailed-reply handler, and a change summary in updates.
4. **Catch up.** Purpose: one list of everything unread, cleared from the keyboard.
   - Layout: a view beside the conversation list (also the side panel's start page). Filter row: All unread, Mentions, Threads I follow; Mark all read. Direct messages first, then channels by latest unread; a channel row shows name, unread count and a mention dot, and expands to its unread messages and threads with new replies (author, snippet, time, count).
   - Seen in the reference: Zulip's Inbox lists only conversations with unread messages, collapses by channel with the state kept in the browser, filters Followed, Standard and All topics, marks a DM, topic or channel read from its row, and opens with Shift+I (`inbox_ui.ts`, `views_util.ts`, `hotkey.ts`).
   - Proposed: arrow keys or j and k move, Enter opens the conversation at an "unread from here" divider, e marks the row read, u marks unread from the focused message, Shift+E marks all read after showing the count; counts refresh from the existing live push.
   - Data: has unread per channel, last message and `last_read_at` per member (`list_channels` in `src/chat.rs`), mentions on each message (`chat.yaml`). Lacks unread for thread replies (the count skips them), thread follow state, and a mark-unread call (extend `POST /channels/{id}/read` with a message id).
5. **Message row and composer.** Purpose: quiet ways to answer, and no lost words.
   - Layout: reaction chips under a message with a "+" at the end; the hover toolbar gains React and Remind me; the Send button gains Schedule send; typing @, # or : opens a completion list; the draft returns when you come back to the channel or thread.
   - Seen in the reference: a chip shows names while the message has at most three reactions in all and a count after that, "You" for yourself (`reactions.ts`); the picker orders emoji by how often they are used (`emoji_frequency.ts`); @ completes people, then all, channel and user groups, with a silent form that does not notify (`composebox_typeahead.ts`); scheduled messages need five minutes' notice (`scheduled_messages.ts`).
   - Proposed: click a chip to toggle; # lists channels and records (reuse `LinkInput`); drafts per channel and per thread kept in the browser; @here counts only people present once presence exists.
   - Data: has people, channels and live push (`Chat.tsx`), a one-minute maintenance tick (`src/lib.rs`). Lacks a reactions table (message, person, emoji; unique per triple; added to `personal_data` in `package.yaml`), a scheduled-message table with a send step, and presence.
6. **Meeting room with recording and consent.** Purpose: record a meeting, tell everyone, and file it.
   - Layout: the current page (title, Invite guests, Copy link, Pop out) plus Record for hosts (event organizer, channel owner, room creator); while recording, everyone sees a red frame and a banner naming who started it; a settings panel for camera (none, blur, image) and microphone (noise filter); guests see a recording notice on the pre-join screen.
   - Seen in the reference: a start and stop call to the LiveKit recording service, one recording per room at a time (409 otherwise), a red inset border and a toast for all (`RecordingIndicator.tsx`), no recording of encrypted rooms (`SettingsMenu.tsx`), mic and camera shortcuts (`KeyboardShortcuts.tsx`). Its own start route carries a warning that it has no authentication.
   - Proposed: on stop, post "Recording saved" with a link in the channel or on the event, with the same access as the meeting; the file name is date and room.
   - Data: has rooms, tokens, host roles (`CalendarEvent.owner`, channel Owner, `comms_meeting.created_by` in `packages/comms/sql/002_meet.sql`), file storage and Drive. Lacks the LiveKit Egress service, a server client for LiveKit's API (Ferrum only signs tokens), a host check on the start and stop routes, consent text; captions need a speech-to-text service (FERRUM-COMMS-002). Whether the noise-filter package works on a self-hosted server: not verified.
7. **Mail filters and storage.** Purpose: rules and quota in Mail settings.
   - Layout: a Filters tab beside Vacation reply and Mail programs: ordered rules with on and off switches and a one-sentence summary; an editor with If (From, To, Subject, List-Id, has attachment, size; all or any) and Then (move to folder, mark read, flag, forward, discard, stop); Show script, read-only; a storage bar under the folder list that turns amber at 90%.
   - Seen in the reference: Stalwart implements JMAP for Sieve scripts and Quotas (README; `crates/jmap/src/sieve`, `quota`); the web admin's own screens: not verified.
   - Proposed: "Always move mail from this sender to..." in the reader menu opens the editor prefilled; drag to reorder; a server error appears on the rule that caused it; the rules are kept as JSON beside the generated script so the editor can reopen them.
   - Data: has the methods through the proxy (`src/webmail.rs`) and per-person mailboxes (`src/hosted.rs`). Lacks the screen, the JSON-beside-script convention, and folder create and rename.

## What Ferrum already does better

- Mail belongs to business records: replies thread onto the lead, ticket or order they answer, "File to a record" in webmail, compose from any app through intents (`mail.yaml`, `Mail.tsx` FileDialog, `app.yaml`). None of the three references has anything like it.
- One identity and permission model: chat membership is enforced in the API (private and direct chats answer 404 to outsiders), with row-level security, an audit trail, and personal-data export and erasure for messages, lists and calendar feeds (`src/chat.rs`, `package.yaml`).
- Meetings follow business access: tokens come from channel membership, event guests or a room link, and outside guests get their own revocable link (`src/meet.rs`). LiveKit Meet hands a token to anyone who names a room and warns that its record route has no authentication (`app/api/connection-details/route.ts`, `app/api/record/start/route.ts`).
- Mailboxes follow the people directory on Stalwart's open edition: created, renamed, locked and restored with the account, shared mailboxes and lists reconciled, passwords sealed (`src/hosted.rs`, `src/groups.rs`). Stalwart's own SCIM provisioning crates carry only the proprietary licence header.
- Webmail is hardened twice: a server-side sanitiser, then a sandboxed frame with its own content policy; remote images load only on request (`src/webmail.rs`, `Mail.tsx` `frameDocument`).
- Calendar stays open to other apps: CalDAV and CardDAV with app passwords, a secret ICS feed, recurrence exceptions (`src/dav.rs`, `src/recur.rs`). Newsletters never send twice and carry one-click unsubscribe headers (`src/newsletter.rs`).
