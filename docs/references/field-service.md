# Field Service: references

**Compared with:** OCA field-service `ad68b39d0218` (`referance/field-service/oca-field-service`, 34 modules, Odoo 19 manifests; reference paths below start there) against Ferrum Field Service `9cb41f28b79f` (`ferrum-app-field-service`, working tree, clean; Ferrum paths start at the ERP folder), the records frame in `ferrum/web` `c04c986d788c` and the platform `ferrum` `81b7f7c8cd38` (both have uncommitted edits, `ListView.tsx` among them; only the files named were read). **Date:** 2026-11-01

## What Ferrum has

- 21 entities (`ferrum-app-field-service/packages/field_service/entities/service.yaml`, `setup.yaml`): requests; work orders with crew, required skills, checklist, parts (serial, batch, warranty), labour, expenses and the customer's signature; appointments; contracts with maintenance plan, SLA and coverage; technicians (skills, working hours in their time zone, home point, van warehouse, rates); installed base with serial and warranty; territories, skills, checklist templates, settings.
- Rules in Rust: an explainable technician ranking and the dispatch board read (`ferrum-app-field-service/src/dispatch.rs`); double booking made impossible by the database (`packages/field_service/sql/001_field_service.sql`, `src/appointment.rs`); SLA verdicts and time-zone working hours (`src/logic.rs`); stock issue on completion and one invoice per job with warranty or contract cover as a discount (`src/work_order.rs`, `src/invoice.rs`); contract visits made once (`src/contract.rs`); a rate-limited public request endpoint and signed tracking links (`src/site.rs`, `src/notify.rs`); six reports (`src/reports.rs`).
- `GET /api/app/field_service/dispatch` returns each technician's days and the unscheduled queue, but nothing draws it: `ferrum-app-field-service/app.yaml` declares no `ui`. People use the generic frame: a board by one select field, a month calendar and a six-week timeline with one bar per record and no lanes (`ferrum/web/src/app/Views.tsx`).
- Not there (grepped `src`, `packages`, `tests`): a site master with access notes and contacts, technician leave or holidays, offline mode, photo or signature capture (the signature is an `attach` field, a plain file input: `ferrum/web/src/app/widgets.tsx`), a link from sales orders or quotations, timesheet or calendar links, a map (none in `ferrum/web/package.json`). Tracked in `ferrum/docs/registry/apps.yaml` (FERRUM-FIELD-002, -003) and `ferrum/docs/registry/backlog.md` (FERRUM-UX-004, FERRUM-INDUSTRY-009).

## Screens to hand-craft

1. **Dispatch board** (dispatcher, all day). Purpose: turn the unscheduled queue into a day's plan and catch problems before the customer does.
   - Layout: top bar with day or week, territory and skill filters; a left rail with the unscheduled queue in the order the endpoint gives (priority, then response due) and an SLA chip on each card; one lane per technician across the day, shaded outside working hours in that technician's time zone, booked and available hours on the lane header; a right drawer for the selected job.
   - Interactions: dragging a card over a lane asks `suggest_technicians` for that slot and shows score and reasons before the drop; dropping calls `schedule` and the server's refusal naming the clashing appointment appears on the lane; drag to move, edge to resize, Alt-drop adds a second technician; travel drawn as a thin bar between blocks from the board's `travel`, and a block flagged when the previous job's end plus travel passes its start; a moved dispatched visit shows "needs dispatch"; keyboard focus moves with arrows, Enter opens, S schedules to the top suggestion; one button dispatches everything scheduled.
   - Data: have all of it (`board`, `suggest` in `ferrum-app-field-service/src/dispatch.rs`; `schedule`, `dispatch` in `src/appointment.rs`). Missing: technician leave, road travel (straight-line at `travel_speed_kmh`, `setup.yaml`), live position.
2. **Technician day** (phone, often without signal). Purpose: run a visit from the phone, including in a basement.
   - Layout: a Today list (window, customer, address with a directions link, phone, SLA chip) and a job page with a sticky bar that shows only the next legal step (Start travel, Arrive, Complete); sections for checklist, parts, labour, expenses, photos and sign-off.
   - Interactions: large tick targets; parts picked from the van's stock with serial scan; a running labour timer; camera for photos and receipts; a signature pad and signer name; a badge such as "3 changes waiting" that syncs on reconnect, the server re-checking checklist, signature, serial counts and stock on arrival; a clear message when the dispatcher moved the job meanwhile.
   - Data: have the actions and child tables (`ferrum-app-field-service/src/work_order.rs`, `service.yaml`), file upload and the van warehouse. Missing: an offline store (FERRUM-FIELD-002; the pattern is POS's `ferrum-app-pos/ui/src/lib/offline.ts`), signature and camera widgets, and an open-ended labour log (`from_time` and `to_time` are both required in `ServiceWorkOrderLabour`).
3. **Work order cockpit** (office). Purpose: judge one job at a glance and finish it.
   - Layout: status stepper with the SLA clock; customer, site and equipment card with warranty and contract badges; visit timeline; checklist progress and photos; costs against billable; the invoice.
   - Interactions: only legal actions shown; earlier jobs on the same equipment inline; invoice preview shows covered lines at a full discount; copy the tracking link.
   - Data: have every field (`service.yaml`: `under_warranty`, `parts_covered`, `labour_covered`, `sla_status`). Missing: a related-records query (the frame has no related-records panel, grepped `ferrum/web/src`); cost fields sit in the `costing` group, so dispatchers must not see them.
4. **Customer tracking page** (public). Purpose: fewer "where is the technician" calls.
   - Layout: one card with status steps, the visit window, technician first names and the on-the-way, arrived and completed times.
   - Interactions: refreshes by itself; a "cannot be home" request that reaches the dispatcher.
   - Data: have what `tracking_view` returns (`ferrum-app-field-service/src/site.rs`); today `page()` prints plain lines under a policy that allows no scripts. Missing: an ETA (travel data exists) and customer actions.

## What Ferrum already does better

- Double booking is impossible whatever writes the data: an exclusion constraint in `ferrum-app-field-service/packages/field_service/sql/001_field_service.sql`. OCA has no overlap check (searched its `*.py` for overlap and double booking).
- Dispatch explains itself: skills, territory, hours in each technician's time zone with DST, travel from the previous job, workload (`src/logic.rs`, `src/dispatch.rs`). OCA lists the workers who hold every required skill and nothing more (`fieldservice_skill/models/fsm_order.py`).
- SLA with response and resolution clocks from the contract or the priority, At Risk and Breached kept current (`src/logic.rs`, `src/work_order.rs`); OCA has no SLA (searched).
- Completion consumes stock with serial and batch checks and invoices once, covered lines at a full discount (`src/work_order.rs`, `src/invoice.rs`); OCA only lists linked invoices (`fieldservice_account/models/fsm_order.py`).
- Contract visits exist exactly once (unique contract plus due date, `src/contract.rs`); customers file and follow requests without an account (`src/site.rs`), where OCA's portal needs a sign-in (`fieldservice_portal/controllers/fsm_order_portal.py`); cost data is hidden by field groups (`perm_group: costing` in `service.yaml`).

## Licence notes

- OCA field-service is AGPL-3.0 (`referance/field-service/oca-field-service/LICENSE`, `referance/INDEX.md`) and every `fieldservice*/__manifest__.py` I read says AGPL-3; its README says a module may differ, so read a manifest before looking at another OCA repository. Ferrum Field Service is AGPL-3.0-only (`ferrum-app-field-service/app.yaml`): compatible, and nothing was pasted (`referance/README.md`).
- Three modules depend on others that are not in this folder (`web_view_google_map`, `web_timeline`, `sign_oca`); their licences are not verified. Any map or timeline library Ferrum picks needs its own licence check, and a Google map brings Google's terms.
