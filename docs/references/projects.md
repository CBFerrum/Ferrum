## Screens to hand-craft

Ordered by value to an app that plans and bills work: daily use, then planning, then iterations and intake. Each loads as a lazy route like `A/ui/src/main.tsx`, so the first download stays inside its size budget. Reuse `@dnd-kit` (already in `ferrum/web/package.json`, used by `F/list/ColumnChooser.tsx`), not native drag events, so touch and keyboard work.

**1. Project board** (replaces the Board tab of `A/ui/src/Project.tsx`)
- Purpose: the screen a team lives in; move work through statuses fast and see everyone's changes live.
- Layout: a toolbar (search, filter chips for assignee, priority, due date and parent; Group by status, assignee or priority; Swimlanes none, assignee or priority) over one column per status: Open, Working, Pending Review, Completed, and Overdue only while a task is in it (as `board()` in `A/ui/src/plan.ts`). Column head: name, count, plus button. Card: number and subject (two lines), diamond for a milestone, priority flag unless Medium, due date (red when late), assignee initials, sub-task count such as 3/5, a lock while a predecessor is open. A click opens a side panel like `F/list/RecordPeek.tsx`; Enter opens the form.
- Interactions: dropping a card in another column sends the new status with the version the card was loaded at (as `F/Views.tsx` does); if a rule refuses (open predecessor, open sub-tasks, closed project) the card springs back and the server's sentence shows. Overdue is not a drop target, it follows the dates. Dropping between two cards sets `rank` to the midpoint of its neighbours, or a fixed step beyond the first or last (`P/issues/issue-layouts/utils.tsx`); under any other sort the column names that sort while a card is dragged over it and says a drop only moves the card between columns (`P/issues/issue-layouts/group-drag-overlay.tsx`). Ours: re-space a column when two ranks come within 1e-6. A box at the foot of each column adds a task in that status (Enter adds and stays open, Esc closes). J and K move focus; S, P, A change status, priority, assignee; Shift+arrow moves a card one column (this replaces the select menu for keyboard and touch). A task has several assignees (`assigned_to` table), so an assignee lane shows it once per assignee and dragging between those lanes is off. `useLiveEntity("Task")` keeps it current; grouping and filters are remembered per person with `usePref`.
- Data: have status, priority, `assigned_to`, `exp_end_date`, `is_milestone`, `parent_task`, `depends_on`. Missing: `rank` (decimal, hidden, `no_copy`; new tasks go last); sub-task counts (a second query grouped by `parent_task`, or counters kept in `TaskHook::after_save`, `A/src/project.rs`); a notice when the 500-task load is cut (`Project.tsx` cuts silently, `F/Views.tsx` says so). Grouping by milestone or cycle needs links Task does not have.

**2. Schedule (Gantt)** (a new tab beside Board)
- Purpose: set dates and links by hand and see the effect before it is saved.
- Layout: left outline about 360 px wide and resizable: tasks as a tree by `parent_task`, columns number and subject, assignee, start, end, %; collapsible, with an add row at the end. Right canvas: two-line header (month over day or week), zoom day, week, month, quarter (keys D, W, M, Q; plus and minus), a Today button (T), weekend shading, today line. One row per task, 32 to 44 px: bar coloured by status with a fill for `progress`, late bars red, done faded, milestones as diamonds, a parent drawn as a summary bar over its children, undated tasks grouped at the bottom, arrows from a predecessor's end to a successor's start. Toggles: critical path, group by assignee.
- Interactions: drag a bar to move it, drag an edge to resize, snapping to days. Hovering an undated row shows a plus under the pointer; a click sets the start to that day and the end one day later, seven at quarter zoom (`P/gantt-chart/helpers/add-block.tsx`). Drag from the dot on a bar's right edge to another bar to link them finish-to-start with no lag; click an arrow to pick its kind (finish-to-start, start-to-start, finish-to-finish, start-to-finish), set lag or delete it (`H/tracker-resources/src/components/gantt/GanttConnectorDot.svelte`, `.../DependencyEditor.svelte`; first version: finish-to-start only). Moving a task that has successors first shows a preview, old positions ghosted, new ones solid, a count of tasks that shift, OK and Cancel (`ConfirmCascadePopup.svelte`). The rule behind it (`gantt/lib/scheduler.ts`): walk the links outward; a successor moves only when its link would break, by at least the days its predecessor moved and keeping its own length, and the walk goes on down the chain; undated successors are skipped and counted; dragging a successor earlier than its link allows pulls its predecessor earlier; the dragged tasks are authoritative; tasks pinned as manual never move; edit rights are checked for every task that would change before anything is written. Dragging a parent moves its dated sub-tasks with it. Alt-drag skips the preview; Esc cancels a drag; arrows nudge a day, Shift+arrows a week; Ctrl or Cmd+Z undoes the last edit from a stack of before and after dates (`gantt/lib/undo-manager.ts`); `?` lists keys; E exports the view as PNG (`gantt/lib/exporter.ts` also writes PDF). Critical path (`gantt/lib/critical-path.ts`): a forward and a backward pass give each task its slack; zero-slack tasks and their links are highlighted.
- Data: have `exp_start_date`, `exp_end_date`, `parent_task`, `is_milestone`, `progress`, `depends_on` (a status rule, not a date rule; cycles refused). Missing: `lag_days` on `TaskDependency`; a pinned flag on Task; a server action that takes the dragged edits and returns the shifts under the existing per-tenant lock, so screen, MCP and bulk edits get one answer (ours; Huly computes in the browser). Weekends and holidays could read HR's `HolidayList` when HR is installed.

**3. My week** (planner; Huly's Planner with Ferrum's time logs)
- Purpose: a person turns open tasks into a plan for the week, sees plan against logged time, and logs from the plan.
- Layout: three panes (`H/time-resources/src/components/PlanView.svelte`). Left: tabs Unplanned, Planned, All, and a source switch Assigned, Created, Following (from Huly's My issues tabs Assigned, Active, Backlog, Created, Subscribed, `HM/tracker/src/index.ts`). Middle: my open tasks grouped by project with due date and hours left (expected minus actual). Right: Monday to Friday grid (weekend toggle), day headers showing planned and logged hours against capacity, blocks stacked below, a strip of logged time per day. Week switcher and This week.
- Interactions: dragging a task onto a day creates an allocation (me, project, task, that day; our default: 2 h or the hours left, whichever is less). Drag a block to another day; drag its bottom edge to change hours in quarter-hour steps (the entity allows 0.25 to 24). A block's Log button opens a time log on that task for the planned hours rounded to a quarter hour (Huly rounds slot time the same way, `IssueToDoDone` in `HS/time-resources/src/index.ts`), asks for start and end, and ticks the block; the server still refuses overlaps. A day over capacity turns amber and shows the sum; it never blocks (the entity text says planners know better). Optional setting: the first plan moves an Open task to Working (Huly does, `OnWorkSlotCreate`). Arrows change week, T returns to this week.
- Data: have, uncommitted, `ResourceAllocation` (person, project, task, dates, hours per day) and `Timesheet` with logs. Missing: a role right for a Projects User to create and edit their own allocations (read-only today, `A/packages/projects/package.yaml`); a done flag; per-person capacity (fixed 40, `A/src/reports.rs`); logged hours by person by day (`timesheet_summary` groups by person, project and activity, so add a report); a list of records I follow (table `record_follower` exists, `ferrum/crates/kernel/migrations/20261030000001_collaboration.sql`; a listing API: not verified).

**4. Team workload and stand-up** (managers; the grid is Ferrum's own, the stand-up is Huly's team agenda)
- Purpose: see weeks ahead who is over or under booked, and run the daily stand-up from one screen.
- Layout: a grid, people down and 8 to 12 weeks across; each cell is planned hours over capacity as a percentage on a heat scale (green to 80, amber to 100, red above; hatched for leave or holiday). Filters: project, portfolio. A top row "Not staffed" lists open tasks with expected hours and no allocation. Clicking a person or cell opens a drawer with Yesterday (time logged, by task) and Today (planned, by task), each with a total and one collapsible section per person (`H/time-resources/src/components/team/agenda/Agenda.svelte`, `PlanPerson.svelte`), plus that week's allocations to edit. Huly's team view is that agenda plus a five-day calendar of planned slots per person (`team/calendar/TeamCalendar.svelte`); neither shows capacity.
- Interactions: arrow keys move between cells, Enter opens the drawer. Drag an allocation chip onto another person's cell to reassign it (changes `person`); drag across empty cells of one person to create an allocation; drag a chip's edges to change its dates. Hover shows hours by project. CSV export of the grid.
- Data: have, uncommitted, `ResourceAllocation` and the `workload` report (person, week, hours, capacity, over; weekends ignored). Missing: per-person weekly capacity and days off, from HR's `HolidayList` and `LeaveApplication` when HR is installed (a connector, as `projects_erp` is for ERP) or a per-person setting otherwise; hours logged per person per day (see My week).

**5. Cycles** (Plane; for teams that work in iterations)
- Purpose: commit to a time-boxed set of tasks, finish it, carry the rest forward and keep an honest record.
- Layout: list with tabs Current, Upcoming, Completed, Draft; status comes from the dates, no dates meaning Draft (`PA/app/views/cycle/base.py`, `PT/constants/src/cycle.ts`). Row: name, dates, lead, progress ring, days left. Detail: screen 1's board and list filtered to the cycle, with a right sidebar of dates, lead, goal, counts by status group, a burn-down or burn-up chart (hours or tasks), and progress by assignee and by priority where a click filters the board (`P/cycles/analytics-sidebar/`, `P/cycles/active-cycle/`).
- Interactions: add tasks with the list's bulk "set a field" on `cycle`, or by dragging from a backlog panel. Complete cycle asks for the next cycle (one already ended is refused), shows "N unfinished move, M done stay", then in one transaction stores a snapshot on the finished cycle (counts, daily series, per-assignee figures) and moves only unfinished tasks (`PA/utils/cycle_transfer_issues.py`). The finished cycle then shows its snapshot, not live counts (`calculateCycleProgress` in `PT/utils/src/cycle.ts`). A task is in at most one cycle (our choice).
- Data: none exists: entity `Cycle` (project, name, start, end, lead, goal, snapshot as json), a `Task.cycle` link, and a daily point per cycle written by the upkeep job in `A/src/lib.rs` (rebuilding the series from record history: not verified). Unfinished means neither Completed nor Cancelled (`CLOSED` in `A/src/logic.rs`).

**6. Intake** (Plane; requests wait here before they are planned)
- Purpose: someone decides on each new request before it enters the plan.
- Layout (`P/inbox/`): two panes. Left: the list with two tabs, Open (pending and snoozed until their day) and Closed (accepted, declined, duplicate), and filters for status, priority and date. Right: the request (title, description, requester, attachments, comments). Header buttons: Accept, Decline, Snooze, Duplicate.
- Interactions: Accept opens a dialog prefilled with project, assignee, priority, cycle and due date, creates the Task and links it. Decline closes it and tells the requester (Plane asks for no reason; a note field is our addition). Snooze picks a day; the request returns to Pending at the start of that day, done by the upkeep job (the same wake-up as `PA/db/models/intake.py`). Duplicate searches existing tasks and closes the request against the one chosen. After a decision the next request opens; ↑ and ↓ walk the list (`P/inbox/content/inbox-issue-header.tsx`). Outsiders submit through a public form (Plane publishes an intake, `PA/db/models/deploy_board.py`).
- Data: none exists: entity `TaskRequest` (title, description, requester email, project, status, `snoozed_till`, `duplicate_of` link to Task, decision note, `task` link). The public form: Forms has record mappings (registry FERRUM-FORMS-001; mapping to a new entity: not verified); Helpdesk tickets are a separate app.

## What Ferrum already does better

- Money: billing method, contract value, rates per person and activity, time to invoice and back on cancel, profitability with ledger expenses (`A/src/billing.rs`, `A/src/reports.rs`, `A/packages/projects_erp/package.yaml`). Plane's models have no time, rate or invoice fields (searched `PA/db/models/`) and its pricing table sells time tracking (`plans.tsx`); Huly's time report carries employee, date, hours and a note only (`TimeSpendReport` in `H/tracker/src/index.ts`).
- Time integrity: spans, overlaps refused, hours derived, billed time locked (`A/src/timesheet.rs`, `A/src/logic.rs`). A Huly report is a date and an amount, so there is nothing to overlap.
- Rules sit on the server for every caller (screen, bulk, REST, MCP): cycle check under an advisory lock, milestone cap, closed-project freeze (`A/src/project.rs`). Huly checks cycles in browser code (`H/tracker-resources/src/components/gantt/lib/scheduler.ts`); a server check: not verified.
- Planning records the references lack: risks with computed exposure, change requests with a different decider, recurring tasks made once per due date, a portfolio overview (`A/src/planning.rs`). Searches of Plane's models and Huly's tracker found none of them.
- Governance: field groups `costing` and `billing`, row ownership, personal-data export, handover of open tasks when a person leaves (`A/packages/projects/package.yaml`), hash-chained record history (`F/FormView.tsx`), the same rules for AI agents over MCP (`A/README.md`).
- Stale-edit safety: board moves and side-panel edits send the version they loaded and reload on conflict (`F/Views.tsx`, `F/list/RecordPeek.tsx`); bulk runs per record with a retry list (`F/list/BulkBar.tsx`).
- Data without a face: the project page opens on a board. Budget, cost, billed, hours against estimate, open risks and pending change requests fit one overview strip (S; fields `proj_*` in `A/packages/projects/package.yaml`). Neither reference has one; do not lose this while copying their boards.
