## What Ferrum has

- **Records.** 21 entities in `packages/helpdesk/entities/support.yaml` (19 in 0.4.1; `EscalationRule` and `ServiceContract` came with `d7ecb04`): a ticket with an append-only `conversation` (internal notes, channel, rating), teams with member caps, assignment rules (CEL condition, round robin or least loaded), SLAs, knowledge base, warranty claims, maintenance schedules and visits. Three roles, personal-data export and erasure, and handover of open tickets when an agent leaves are declared in `packages/helpdesk/package.yaml:26-89`; `packages/helpdesk_erp` adds the ticket's sales invoice.
- **SLA engine** (`src/sla.rs`, `src/ticket.rs:152`): weekday hours, holidays, fixed UTC offset, pausing statuses. Deadlines are recomputed from the status timeline on every save and every few minutes by `maintenance` (`src/lib.rs:41`); the most specific agreement wins (customer, group, type).
- **Since 0.4.1 (`d7ecb04`).** Escalation rules fire by level, once per ticket, on At Risk or Breached: reassign, move team, raise priority, notify (`src/ticket.rs:711-805`). Service contracts stamp Covered, Expired or Exhausted on new tickets and swap in their SLA; they inform and never refuse (`src/ticket.rs:416-448`). Tests: `tests/helpdesk_it.rs:821-887`. Neither entity is in the sidebar list (`packages/helpdesk/package.yaml:20-22`), and the ticket view shows neither entitlement nor escalation level; only the full form does (`packages/helpdesk/entities/support.yaml:199-200`).
- **Interface** (`ui/src`): inbox of four live queues sorted by urgency (`ui/src/Home.tsx`, `ui/src/inbox.ts`); ticket page with thread, reply or internal-note composer, a status change that asks for the resolution, priority, assign to me, due labels, version-checked saves (`ui/src/Ticket.tsx`); portal "Your tickets" with replies, new ticket and rating, rate limited (`ui/src/Portal.tsx`, `src/site.rs`); the rest through the records frame (`ui/src/main.tsx`). Route lists: FH 21 (inbox, ticket, dashboard, KB, customers, contacts, calls, search, notifications), Ferrum 9, CW 6 and incomplete (`referance/_inventory/helpdesk__frappe-helpdesk.txt`, `referance/_inventory/ferrum__helpdesk.txt`, `referance/_inventory/helpdesk__chatwoot.txt`).
- **Inherited frame.** Filter chips, per-user saved views, column chooser, bulk bar, CSV export and a peek panel with inline edit (`web/src/app/ListView.tsx`, `web/src/app/list/`); board by any select such as status, calendar, timeline (`web/src/app/Views.tsx`); Discussion with comments, @mentions, tags, attachments, follow and assignees (`web/src/app/Collab.tsx`, exported at `web/packages/records/src/index.ts:22`); Activity with hash-chain check (`web/src/app/FormView.tsx:865`); the bar's Cmd/Ctrl-K finds apps, records and `shell.search` results (`server/shell/runtime.js:452`). Of the optional contribution points Helpdesk uses `shell.work`, `shell.portal` and notification kinds (`app.yaml:32-42`), and none of `shell.search`, `shell.intent`, `shell.card` or a widget.
- **Reports** (`src/reports.rs`): ticket summary, SLA compliance, response and resolution times (average, median, 90th percentile), agent workload, satisfaction; each limited to the caller's team scope.

## Screens to hand-craft

### 1. Ticket workspace (replaces `ui/src/Ticket.tsx`)
- **Purpose and layout.** Answer one ticket without leaving the keyboard. Header: `Inbox > TKT-2026-000123`, previous and next arrows for the list it was opened from, avatars of colleagues viewing, status menu, a "..." menu (merge, split, copy link). Main column: feed filter (All, Replies, Notes); the feed (customer left, agent right, internal notes tinted with an "Internal" chip, one-line grey events for status, assignee, priority, SLA breach, escalation level); the composer docked at the bottom. Right rail, 320 px, collapsible: customer card (name, email, open and recent tickets, contract with an Entitlement chip, warranty claims, invoices); two SLA cards "First response" and "Resolution" that rest in plain grey ("Due in 3 h") and take colour only when something changed (green "Fulfilled in 3 h 20 min", red "Overdue by 59 min" while open or "Failed by 20 min" when met late, blue "On hold"; hover: due time, paused since, answered or resolved on, delay in business and in calendar hours); fields (assignee, team, type, priority, tags); files.
- **Interactions.** Composer switches Reply and Internal note. `/` opens the saved-reply picker: search box, scope filter that is remembered (All, Personal, My teams, Everyone), most used first on an empty query, a preview rendered for this ticket, Enter inserts; a reply with actions shows them as chips that run after sending and can be removed first; a variable that cannot be filled blocks sending. `@` in a note mentions and notifies. Paste or drop files. Ctrl/Cmd+Enter sends. "Send and set" picks Resolved (asks for the resolution as today), On hold until (an hour, tomorrow 09:00, next Monday, a date, next customer reply) or leaves it open. The draft is kept per ticket and warns on leaving. Keys outside inputs: R reply, C internal note, S status, P priority, A assign, G tags, Shift+> and Shift+< next and previous, ? sheet. A 409 shows the colleague's change and keeps the draft; a change by a colleague while the page is open raises a toast ("Priya set priority to Urgent"). Presence: avatar stack and "Priya is typing".
- **Provenance.** The three-part layout, single keys, next and previous, saved-reply picker and snooze options come from FH and CW (rows 2, 5-7 and 11 of the table); the SLA card wording is FH's `desk/src/composables/useSLA.ts`; "Send and set", the entitlement chip, escalation events and the note-mention notice are ours.

## What Ferrum already does better

- **SLA arithmetic you can trust.** Deadlines come from the status timeline each time, so a holiday edit, priority change, reopening or pause gives what the ticket would have had from the start; night shifts, fixed offsets and adjacent pauses are unit-tested (`src/ticket.rs:5-7`, `src/sla.rs`). FH keeps running hold counters instead (`on_hold_since`, `total_hold_time` in `helpdesk/helpdesk/doctype/hd_ticket/hd_ticket.json`). One enabled agreement per criteria set, contract SLA first (`src/ticket.rs:135,255-265`).
- **Escalation and entitlement.** Rules fire once per level even with several replicas, and are audited (`src/ticket.rs:752-805`); contracts count tickets in a period. FH has neither (escalation appears only as a search keyword, `desk/src/components/command-palette/ticketCommands.ts:89`); CW can approximate escalation with delayed automation rules (`app/models/automation_rule.rb`, `execution_delay`) but keeps its SLA policies under `enterprise/`, and has no contracts.
- **Fair assignment under load.** The rule row is locked, so simultaneous tickets take turns; per-agent caps, CEL conditions, agents confined to their teams (`src/ticket.rs:303-388`, tests at `src/ticket.rs:849-903`).
- **An honest record.** Messages are append-only and server-stamped, a closed ticket is final, customers cannot write internal notes, two agents answering at once is a conflict, not an overwrite (`src/ticket.rs:578-620`, `ui/src/Ticket.tsx:60-65`, `tests/helpdesk_it.rs:882-887`).
- **Portal boundary.** Internal notes never leave, agents show by name, actions run as the system only after the ownership check, rate limited (`src/site.rs:100-285`).
- **Warranty and maintenance tied to serial numbers and deliveries** (`src/warranty.rs`, `src/visits.rs`): absent from both references (no warranty code in either).
- **Governance.** Reports honour team scope (`src/reports.rs`), personal data and handover are declared (`packages/helpdesk/package.yaml:26,86`), and agents act through the same REST and MCP permissions as people.
