# Bookings: references

**Compared with:** calcom/cal.com at `f972feefa137` (`referance/bookings/cal.com`, MIT; its README calls the tree Cal.diy) and Ferrum Bookings 0.1.1 plus the unreleased repeat series at `eec1009fbedd` (`ferrum-app-bookings`, branch `bookings/series`, working tree clean). **Date:** 2026-11-01

Paths: cal.com paths start at `referance/bookings/cal.com/`. Ferrum paths are inside `ferrum-app-bookings` unless they start with `ferrum/` (the platform at `2990be1f8889`; its frame `ferrum/web` is at `00166d3ba92b` with a few uncommitted edits, so only committed behaviour is claimed), `ferrum-app-comms/` (`c88bbbc86c7d`) or `ferrum-app-commerce/` (`bdc6efe9afca`). `referance/_inventory/` holds no route list for these apps, so absences below come from directory listings and grep.

## What Ferrum has

- **Records (12 entities, `packages/bookings/entities/bookings.yaml`):** services (a one-to-one appointment or a class with seats, buffers, price, intake questions), staff in their own time zone with split hours, time off, rooms and equipment with a capacity, settings, and bookings with actions (confirm, reschedule, cancel, complete, no-show, invoice, guest link, repeat, cancel series).
- **Engine and guarantees:** one pure availability engine decides both the slots offered and the bookings taken, DST-correct (`src/engine.rs`); a database exclusion constraint refuses overlapping staff time (`packages/bookings/sql/001_bookings.sql`); waitlists refill class seats (`src/booking.rs`, `promote`); repeat series (`src/series.rs`); notices and reminders go out once (`src/notify.rs`); signed staff `.ics` feeds (`src/ics.rs`); three reports (`src/reports.rs`).
- **Guest side is an API:** services, slots in the guest's zone, book, manage, reschedule, cancel and `.ics`, throttled, with a honeypot (`src/site.rs`). No page is rendered: the only HTML in the crate is email (`src/notify.rs`), and `BookingSettings.manage_page_url` points guests at a page the operator builds.
- **No `ui/`:** records open in the generic frame at `/data/bookings/`: list, board by status, a month calendar and day-bar timeline (`ferrum/web/src/app/Views.tsx`, `ferrum/web/src/lib/views.ts`). The month grid has no time axis and offers "New" only for date fields, not datetimes (`Views.tsx:210`), so no screen shows a day's schedule.
- **Tracker:** deposits and refunds are missing and calendar sync is partial (`ferrum/docs/capabilities.md:129-130`).

## Screens to hand-craft

1. **Guest booking page.**
   - *Purpose and layout:* let a guest book, change and cancel without an account, served on a `site:bookings` host the way Commerce serves its shop. The Booker: a left panel with the service (title, duration, price, location, staff, time-zone select), a month calendar with bookable days enabled, the day's times with seats left, then the form, then a success card (`apps/web/modules/bookings/components/Booker.tsx`, `AvailableTimes.tsx`, `SeatsAvailabilityText.tsx`). Phones stack the same steps.
   - *Interactions:* the first bookable day is selected and a full month moves on to the next (`apps/web/modules/bookings/components/DatePicker.tsx`); a 12 or 24 hour switch (`AvailableTimeSlots.tsx`); the list refreshes on window focus and every few minutes, and a time taken meanwhile disables Confirm with a message (`packages/features/bookings/Booker/README.md`); a full class offers the waitlist; answers are checked per field (the API already returns field errors); the success card carries Add to calendar and the reschedule and cancel links with their windows.
   - *Data:* services, slots (with seats left and staff), book, manage and `.ics` exist (`packages/bookings/README.md`); holding a time while typing does not (row 7 of the table).
2. **Schedule board (day and week).**
   - *Purpose and layout:* the front desk's view of who is where. A time grid in quarter-hour rows, one column per staff member (day) or per day (week, one staff filter), optionally per room; blocks sized to each booking, buffers shaded, time off hatched, working hours as the lit background, a line at the current time, a class session as one block with seats taken of total; chips for staff, service, status and resource; a side sheet.
   - *Interactions:* cal.com draws the week on a quarter-hour grid in the viewer's zone, colours by event type and opens a sheet on click (`BookingCalendarView.tsx`); here a click on an empty cell also starts a booking with staff and start filled in, and (our addition) dragging a block calls `reschedule` and snaps back with the server's reason if it refuses. Pending and waitlisted bookings sit in a side rail.
   - *Data:* `Booking` (`start_time`, `end_time`, staff, resource, seats, status), `StaffTimeOff` and `BookingStaff.working_hours` exist; buffers are on the service. The frame's month grid cannot do this (`ferrum/web/src/app/Views.tsx`).
3. **Booking sheet with a slot picker.**
   - *Purpose and layout:* a side sheet over the board or list with Info and History tabs and the sections When, Who, Where, Answers, Billing, with actions by status in the footer (`apps/web/modules/bookings/components/BookingDetailsSheet.tsx`).
   - *Interactions:* up and down arrows move to the previous and next booking and Enter joins the meeting link (`apps/web/modules/bookings/lib/bookingSheetKeyboardHandler.ts`); Reschedule shows a day strip and real times for that service and seat count instead of a typed datetime; Cancel asks for a reason and says whether the guest is told; Copy guest link; Create invoice shows the amount; History is the audit chain.
   - *Data:* the actions, `guest_link` and `find_slots` exist; `find_slots` takes no booking id, so a booking's own time is not offered back to it (`src/setup.rs:72-73`), a small parameter. Asking the guest for a new time is new.
4. **Hours and exceptions editor.**
   - *Purpose and layout:* per staff member, Monday to Sunday rows with a switch, time ranges, add range and a "copy times to" popover; beside them the time zone, date overrides chosen on a calendar dialog (set hours or mark unavailable) and time off (`packages/features/schedules/components/ScheduleComponent.tsx`, `DateOverrideInputDialog.tsx`).
   - *Interactions:* overlap and `HH:MM` errors inline, from the rules the server already applies (`packages/bookings/README.md`, "Setup"); a week strip previews the result; saving time off lists the bookings it collides with (the count is kept as `conflicting_bookings`) and offers Reschedule.
   - *Data:* working-hour rows, `StaffTimeOff` and `conflicting_bookings` exist; date overrides do not (an engine change, `src/engine.rs`).
5. **Why not this time?**
   - *Purpose and layout:* a support tool in the shape of cal.com's Troubleshooter: pick a service, staff member and week; the grid layers working hours, time off, bookings with buffers, a full resource and the notice and advance windows (`apps/web/modules/troubleshooter/components/Troubleshooter.tsx`).
   - *Interactions:* click a time and get one sentence: outside hours, inside the buffer of a named booking, room full, too soon.
   - *Data:* the engine holds every input (`src/engine.rs`, `src/calendar.rs`) but returns slots, not reasons; an explain function is new.

## What Ferrum already does better

- Overlaps are refused by the database too: an exclusion constraint on staff time, under row locks (`packages/bookings/sql/001_bookings.sql`). cal.com checks in application code (`packages/features/bookings/lib/conflictChecker/checkForConflicts.ts`) and reserves slots briefly (`packages/features/bookings/Booker/README.md`); grep finds no `EXCLUDE USING` in `packages/prisma`.
- Rooms and equipment with a capacity shared by services (`BookingResource`, `src/engine.rs`); `packages/prisma/schema.prisma` has no resource model.
- Waitlists that fill themselves when seats free up (`src/booking.rs`, `promote`); grep finds no waitlist in `schema.prisma` or `packages/features/bookings/lib/handleSeats`.
- Completed and No Show as booking statuses, with attendance and staff-utilisation reports (`src/reports.rs`); cal.com's statuses are cancelled, accepted, rejected, pending and awaiting host (`schema.prisma:843`), and its Insights are not in this tree.
- A paid booking becomes an ERP sales invoice at the booked price, once, for a customer made from the guest (`make_sales_invoice`, `src/booking.rs`); cal.com keeps a payment record per booking (`Payment` in `schema.prisma`) and no books.
