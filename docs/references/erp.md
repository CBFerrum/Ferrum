# ERP: references

**Compared with:** ERPNext `b23972f80f5c` (frappe/erpnext `develop`, 17.0.0-dev; `referance/erp/erpnext` links to `upstream/erpnext` and is not in `INDEX.md`, so the commit comes from its `.git`; reference paths below start at `referance/erp/erpnext/erpnext/`) against Ferrum ERP `993017fc502a` (`erp-platform`, working tree, clean; Ferrum paths start at the ERP folder), the records frame in `ferrum/web` `9ecf5bfc8404` and the platform `ferrum` `2a701db8901a` (both have uncommitted edits, `ListView.tsx` among them; only the files named were read). **Date:** 2026-11-01

## What Ferrum has

- Five packages, 35 entities (`erp-platform/packages/*/entities/*.yaml`): accounting (universal journal, journal and payment entries with open-item allocation, tax templates and rates, fiscal years), selling (orders, delivery notes, invoices, pricing rules), buying (orders, receipts, supplier invoices, three-way match), inventory, manufacturing (multi-level BOMs with revisions, work orders, MRP). The engine is `erp-platform/src/` (`ledger.rs`, `stock.rs`, `pricing.rs`, `trade.rs`, `manufacturing.rs`, `localization.rs`).
- Four operational add-ons, 78 entities: Finance (bank import and reconciliation, budgets, period closing, dunning, subscriptions, payment terms, advances), Procurement, Stock Operations (reconciliation, landed cost, picking, quality, variants, bundles), Manufacturing Operations (workstations, routings, job cards, production plans, subcontracting) in `erp-platform/addons/`; 32 reports in the five packages and these four add-ons; 26 countries' rules in the other add-ons on the seam in `src/localization.rs`.
- The interface is almost all generic: `erp-platform/ui/src/` holds routing (`main.tsx`), a home page taken from the frame (`home.ts`) and the portal's invoices (`Invoices.tsx`); every other screen is the frame (`ferrum/web/src/app/ListView.tsx`, `FormView.tsx`, `Views.tsx`).
- Gaps already tracked: consolidation's translation reserve, minority interest and mirrored intercompany invoices, accruals and deferred revenue, supplier portal, dimensions limited to cost centres (FERRUM-ERP-003, -005, -006, -011 in `ferrum/docs/registry/apps.yaml`); no shop floor (FERRUM-INDUSTRY-006 in `ferrum/docs/registry/backlog.md`).

## Screens to hand-craft

1. **Bank reconciliation workbench** (accountant, weekly). Purpose: clear a bank account's statement lines against payments and journals fast and without mistakes.
   - Layout: account and period on top with three figures (statement balance, ledger balance, difference); unreconciled lines on the left (date, description, reference, deposit or withdrawal, unallocated, status); the selected line's ranked matches on the right, with modes Match, Create payment, Create journal.
   - Interactions: j and k move through lines, Enter accepts the top match when its amount is exact; tick several vouchers with editable amounts for a split or partial match; "accept all exact matches" with a preview; undo per line; drop a CSV to import with a mapping preview and a count of skipped duplicates; the difference updates live.
   - Data: have lines, allocations, the suggestion fields (exact amount, reference match, party match, days apart, score), `make_payment_entry`, `make_journal_entry`, `unreconcile` (`erp-platform/addons/finance/packages/finance/entities/bank.yaml`, `src/bank.rs`) and the statement report. Missing: rules, the bank's own balance per line, OFX or MT940 import.
2. **Party allocation** (receivables and payables clerk). Purpose: apply one customer's or supplier's unallocated payments, credits and advances to open invoices in one sitting.
   - Layout: party and account on top; two tables side by side, money to apply (payments, credit notes, journals) and open invoices (outstanding, due date, age); an allocation drawer.
   - Interactions: oldest-first auto-allocate; edit each pairing; the exchange difference shown per pairing before confirming; confirm posts every pairing or none.
   - Data: have open items with over-allocation refused by the database (`erp-platform/src/ledger.rs`, `allocate`), `unallocated_amount` on payment entries (`src/accounting.rs`) and the advance logic (`addons/finance/src/advances.rs`). Missing: a party-level list endpoint and credit-note and journal pairing.
3. **Accounts home** (finance lead; the model for the other module homes). Purpose: a one-minute morning check.
   - Layout: cash by bank account, receivables and payables ageing bars, due this week, unreconciled line count, drafts waiting to be submitted, a period-close checklist, profit and loss by month.
   - Interactions: every figure opens its filtered list; company and date switch on top.
   - Data: have receivables, payables, budget variance and bank statement reports (`erp-platform/src/reports.rs`, `addons/finance/src/reports.rs`); draft counts are plain list filters. Studio could render the cards and charts; the frame's home is only a module list.
4. **Shop floor** (operator at a tablet, supervisor at a desk). Purpose: start, pause and finish operations without opening forms.
   - Layout: a manager board of work orders by state with a chip per operation; an operator view per workstation with the current job, a large timer, material status, quantity and process-loss entry.
   - Interactions: keyboard first; the timer survives a reload; an operation cannot finish more than the one before it (the server rule in `erp-platform/addons/mfg-ops/src/job_card.rs`); live updates.
   - Data: have job cards, work order operations and workstation capacity (`erp-platform/addons/mfg-ops/`). Missing: open-ended time logs and start and pause actions.

## What Ferrum already does better

- The books cannot be edited into inconsistency: the database refuses unbalanced entries, edits, deletes and postings into closed periods (`erp-platform/packages/accounting/sql/001_ledger.sql`), and stock moves are append-only (`packages/inventory/sql/001_stock.sql`). ERPNext has folders for reposting ledgers and monitoring ledger health (`accounts/doctype/repost_accounting_ledger/`, `ledger_health_monitor/`, `stock/doctype/repost_item_valuation/`); Ferrum chose never to repost, which means a backdated document does not re-value later ones (`ferrum/docs/limits.md`, FERRUM-ERP-009).
- Countries are add-ons on one seam: 26 of them, many with e-invoicing (`erp-platform/src/localization.rs`, `addons/`), against six country folders in `regional/` (australia, italy, south_africa, turkey, united_arab_emirates, united_states; other countries elsewhere in the tree are not verified).
- A supplier answers a request for quotation from a signed, expiring link with no account (`erp-platform/addons/procurement/src/rfq.rs`); ERPNext's page needs a signed-in portal user (`templates/pages/rfq.py`).
- Tax rates are data that a country's add-on prefers over built-in rates, so a new rate needs no release (`erp-platform/CHANGELOG.md`, ERP 0.4.1).
