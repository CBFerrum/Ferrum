## What Ferrum has

- **Records** (`crm/packages/crm/entities/{pipeline,masters,quotation}.yaml`): 13 entities in the working tree (the README says 12): Lead, Opportunity with items and competitors, Activity (call, meeting, email, task, note), Quotation with items, Prospect, Campaign, Territory, SalesStage (probability, Won/Lost outcome), LostReason, QuotationApprovalRule. Contacts, addresses, customers and items are the platform's (`plat/packages/contacts`, `parties`, `products`).
- **Logic** (`crm/src/`): one lead per email under a lock, qualification, one-step conversion to customer, contact and address that re-parents the lead's deals, quotes and activities (`lead.rs`); stage, status and probability kept consistent, weighted amounts in company currency, win on order, reopen on cancel (`opportunity.rs`); ERP-priced quotations, expiry job, loss reasons (`quotation.rs`); a done call stamps the lead (`activity.rs`).
- **Reports** (`crm/src/reports.rs`): pipeline by stage, lead funnel by source, campaign, territory or owner, campaign cost per lead and ROI, quotation register. All honour own-records scope.
- **Its interface** (`crm/ui/src/Home.tsx`, `pipeline.ts`): one hand-written page: open deals as read-only stage columns (500 deals, 8 cards per stage), my 10 next open activities, the 8 newest leads. Every other screen is the shared frame.
- **The shared frame** (`web/src/app/`): filter chips, per-person saved views, column chooser, bulk bar, CSV export, board (select fields only), month calendar, timeline, side panel with inline edit (`list/RecordPeek.tsx`), form tabs Discussion (assign, tags, files, @mentions, follow) and Activity (hash-chained audit), print and email of a document; Ctrl/Cmd-K finder in the platform bar.
- **Next door:** Comms keeps a `Communication` log keyed to any record, templates, and `compose` and `schedule` intents (`comms/packages/comms/entities/mail.yaml`, `comms/app.yaml`); Forms turns a response into a Lead (`ferrum-app-forms/src/mapping.rs`); Helpdesk shows how an app builds its own record page and work feed (`ferrum-app-helpdesk/ui/src/Ticket.tsx`, `app.yaml`).
- **Uncommitted in CRM:** Lead `mergeable` with `duplicate_fields`, quotation approval rules and an Approve action, a `sales_forecast` report. By reading: the forecast and the rules master are missing from the module `items` (`crm/packages/crm/package.yaml`), so no menu reaches them, and the unit test `catalog_names_entities_and_params` (`crm/src/reports.rs`) still expects four reports. Not run.

## Screens to hand-craft

Build in `crm/ui/src/` on `@ferrum/client` (`api`, `shell().intent`), `@ferrum/kit` (`useLiveEntity`, `useCanIntent`, `Dialog`, `toast`) and `@ferrum/records` (`CollabPanel`, `Composer`, `LinkInput`, `useEntityMeta`, `can`). Model: `ferrum-app-helpdesk/ui/src/Ticket.tsx`. Keep filters in the URL as the frame does (`f.<field>`, `web/src/lib/filters.ts`) so saved views and links keep working.

**1. Pipeline board (becomes `/crm/`)**
- **Purpose:** a rep or manager moves deals through stages and sees what the pipeline is worth, at a glance.
- **Layout:** header with title, owner switch (Mine, Team, a person), closing-period filter, search, New deal. One column per enabled open `SalesStage` in `stage_order`; Won and Lost drop zones appear at the right edge while dragging. Column header: stage name, probability, count, sum of `base_amount`, sum of `base_weighted_amount`, and a "+" that opens quick-add (title, party, amount) with the stage preset. Card: title, party, amount in company currency, expected close (red when past), owner initials, `next_step`, and a footer of open-activity counts that deep-link to the deal page. Phones stack the columns as collapsible sections.
- **Interactions:** a drop sends one PATCH with `If-Match` (`api.patch(path, body, {ifMatch})`), moves the card at once and puts it back with a toast on 409, 412 or refusal; the server sets status, probability and weighted amount from the stage (`crm/src/opportunity.rs`), so redraw from the response. Drop on Lost opens a dialog (Lost Reason, detail) and sends stage, reason and detail in one PATCH; cancel puts the card back. Drop on Won confirms. Keep a move menu for touch and keyboards, as `board-move` does (`web/src/app/Views.tsx`). Focused card: arrows move between cards and columns, `x` selects, Shift+`x` extends, Enter opens the peek panel, Esc clears; a selection drags as a group and shows the bulk bar. `useLiveEntity("Opportunity")` keeps totals current. Per-column "load more" (the list API caps at 500).
- **Data:** have `Opportunity` (title, party_name, sales_stage, base_amount, base_weighted_amount, probability, expected_close_date, assigned_to, next_step, status), `SalesStage` (order, probability, outcome, disabled), `LostReason`. Missing: a `position` for order inside a column (today sorted by close date, `crm/ui/src/pipeline.ts`); `stage_changed_on` and `last_activity_on` for "days in stage" and a stale dot (set in `crm/src/opportunity.rs` and `activity.rs`); open-activity counts per deal (the list API has no group-by: one `Activity` query grouped on the client, or counts added to a report). The same component serves Leads by status: Converted opens the convert dialog, Lost and Junk ask for a reason.

**2. Deal and lead workspace**
- **Purpose:** everything about one lead or deal on one page, with logging and mailing in place. The same page serves Prospect, Customer and Contact with Deals and Contacts tabs.
- **Layout:** two panes. Left: a stage strip (open stages as chips, click to move; Won and Lost buttons), tabs Timeline, Mail, Calls and meetings, Tasks, Notes, Quotes, Files, and a composer bar fixed under the timeline (Reply, Note, Log call, Task, Schedule). Right, resizable: summary fields with inline edit (the pattern of `web/src/app/list/RecordPeek.tsx`), contacts (collapsible rows, primary badge, "+" to add existing or create), the account with its other deals, quotes with status. Register it in `crm/app.yaml` `shell.open` as `crm/d/{id}`, like Helpdesk's `helpdesk/t/{id}`.
- **Interactions:** newest first, with an Upcoming group on top (open tasks and meetings). The feed merges audit events ("stage changed from A to B"), Activity rows, Communication threads folded by `thread_key`, comments, files and quote events; the tabs filter it; a converted lead's history shows on its deal. The composer expands and resizes, Ctrl/Cmd+Enter submits, `@` mentions people (`Composer`). Reply and Schedule call `shell().intent({action: "compose", type: "email"})` and `{action: "schedule", type: "event", data: {record: {entity, id}}}`, hidden when `useCanIntent` says no handler. A task ticks off in place (`complete` action). Rename the form's audit tab "History" here so it does not clash with CRM Activities.
- **Data:** have Activity (lead, opportunity, customer, contact), Communication (`reference_entity`, `reference_id`, `thread_key`, `direction`), `/api/v1/{E}/{id}/audit`, `/comments`, `/attachments`, Quotation (`opportunity`, `lead`), Contact (`lead`, `customer`). Missing: one feed endpoint (else five queries merged on the client; an app endpoint as in `ferrum-app-helpdesk/src/site.rs`) and a contacts child table on Opportunity with a primary flag.

**3. Today**
- **Purpose:** what to do next, in order, without opening lists.
- **Layout:** groups Overdue, Today, Next 7 days, No date. Row: tick box, type icon, subject, the lead or deal, due time, owner. Below: Leads to chase (follow-up due, or New with no contact for two days) and Deals needing a next step (open, no `next_step`, or follow-up due). A mini month on the right plots `due_at` (the frame's calendar already can).
- **Interactions:** tick completes (`complete` action; the server marks the lead contacted, `crm/src/activity.rs`); snooze menu (tomorrow, next week, pick) patches `due_at`; `j` and `k` move, `c` completes, `s` snoozes; "+ Task" takes subject, date and link in one line; a manager can view a rep's list and also sees quotations waiting for approval (`needs_approval`, no `approved_by`, the role named by `QuotationApprovalRule.approver_role`) with an Approve button. The same list feeds Home's My work through a `shell.work` provider, and a reminder `shell.notification` kind is sent from `maintenance()` (`crm/src/lib.rs`, must be idempotent), as Helpdesk does for SLA.
- **Data:** have Activity (`due_at`, `status`, `assigned_to`, type), `next_follow_up` and `next_step` on Lead and Opportunity, Lead `last_contacted_on`. Missing: any reader of them, Opportunity `last_activity_on`, Activity priority (Frappe tasks have Low, Medium, High: `fr/crm/fcrm/doctype/crm_task/`). Assignments already reach My work through `/api/work`; activities do not.

**4. Duplicates and merge**
- **Purpose:** one record per person; finish the screen for the kernel's merge.
- **Layout:** left, groups from `GET /api/v1/Lead/_duplicates` (field, shared value, count). Right, the group's records as columns: name, organization, email, phone, owner, status, created, counts of deals, quotes and activities; a "Keep this one" radio on each header (default the oldest), a reason box, Merge. Also reachable from the list's bulk bar with two rows selected.
- **Interactions:** "use this value" copies a field onto the kept record by PATCH before merging (the kernel keeps the survivor's own values); the footer says what moves ("3 deals, 2 quotes, 5 activities, comments, files, tags") and that a merge is not undone; Merge posts `{duplicate, reason}` to `/{id}/merge` once per duplicate and shows `links_moved`.
- **Data:** have it all server-side (`plat/crates/kernel/src/merge.rs`; Lead `duplicate_fields` are email, mobile, phone). The call needs delete permission and lists only what the caller may read. Missing: counts per duplicate (list queries by the link), and Contact and Prospect do not opt in.

**5. Import wizard (belongs in the frame's ListView, for every app)**
- **Purpose:** bring a spreadsheet of leads or contacts in without a developer.
- **Layout:** a dialog in five steps: file (CSV or XLSX), sheet and header row, match columns (auto-match by label and name; skip), review (first 20 rows, counts, problems per row), result (created, failed, failures as a CSV).
- **Interactions:** validate on the client from field metadata (type, options, required) and flag rows that repeat a `duplicate_fields` value; send chunks of 500 and keep going past row errors; "Import only valid rows".
- **Data:** have `_import` (5,000 rows, per-row errors), the `import` permission, entity metadata. Missing: a dry run, link-by-title lookup (rows need ids), a spreadsheet parser in the web build (not verified), the Import button (none in `web/src/app/ListView.tsx`).

## What Ferrum already does better

- **Quotes.** Priced and taxed by the shared selling engine; an approval binds to the approved total and discount, so any change voids it, and nobody approves their own, administrators included; expiry job, unit conversion, orders written back to lines (`crm/src/quotation.rs`). Twenty's standard objects include no quote or product (`tw/twenty-shared/src/metadata/constants/standard-object.constant.ts`); Frappe CRM hands quoting to ERPNext: a Create Quotation form script and a read-only list of the deal's quotes (`fr/crm/fcrm/doctype/erpnext_crm_settings/erpnext_crm_settings.py`, `fr/frontend/src/components/Activities/QuotationsList.vue`).
- **Deal integrity on the server.** Stage, status and probability cannot disagree; a won deal follows its order and reopens when the order is cancelled; the last lost quote loses the deal (`crm/src/opportunity.rs`). Twenty's stage is a plain five-value select with no probability field (`tsa/utils/field-metadata/compute-opportunity-standard-flat-field-metadata.util.ts`).
- **Conversion in one transaction** that re-parents deals, quotes, activities, contacts and addresses, and refuses a second conversion (`crm/src/lead.rs`).
- **Own-records security** by row-level security, carried into reports (`crm/src/reports.rs`, `scope`) and tested (`crm/tests/crm_it.rs`, "a rep's pipeline is their own").
- **Merge semantics:** old ids keep answering with the survivor, comments, files, tags and followers move, legal holds and cross-company merges are refused (`plat/crates/kernel/src/merge.rs`).
- **Verifiable history:** the Activity tab says whether the audit hash chain is intact (`web/src/app/FormView.tsx`, `AuditPanel`). People changes: personal-data export and erasure, and handover of open work when someone leaves (`crm/packages/crm/package.yaml`).
- **One shared catalogue and customer record.** The CRM links the platform's Item, Customer, Contact and Address directly (`crm/packages/crm/package.yaml`, `depends`); Frappe CRM keeps a CRM Product that mirrors ERPNext Items, with a sync-issue log (`fr/crm/integrations/erpnext/item.py`, `fr/crm/fcrm/doctype/crm_product_sync_issue/`).
- **Frame details to keep when hand-crafting:** a move menu on every board card for touch and keyboards (`web/src/app/Views.tsx`), version-checked inline edits that refuse stale writes (`web/src/app/list/RecordPeek.tsx`), bulk edits with per-record results and retry (`web/src/app/list/BulkBar.tsx`), phone card layout (`web/src/app/ListView.tsx`), filters held in the URL.
