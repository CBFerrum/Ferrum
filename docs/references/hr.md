# HR: references

**Compared with:** FHR = frappe/hrms `ffadbbfa51ce` (`referance/hr/frappe-hrms`, GPL-3.0), against ferrum-app-hr 0.3.4 at `bfac32b` (training programs, sessions and records: uncommitted when I started, committed during the review; no version bump or CHANGELOG entry yet) and the shared frame in ferrum/web (`web/`) on ux/records-frame at `00166d3` (filter chips and peek panel, committed during the review). Path shorthand: bare paths are in ferrum-app-hr, where `people.yaml`, `attendance.yaml` and `talent.yaml` are in `packages/hr/entities/` and `Home.tsx` is `ui/src/Home.tsx`; FHR doctype folders are in `hrms/hr/doctype/` (payroll ones in `hrms/payroll/doctype/`). The payroll law of any country is out of scope here and is never listed as a gap to close. **Date:** 2026-11-01

## What Ferrum has

- **Records.** 57 entity definitions in 0.3.4 plus 4 training ones from `bfac32b`, in four packages: `hr` and `payroll`, which need only the core (`packages/hr/package.yaml:5`, `packages/payroll/package.yaml:5`), and the connectors `hr_erp` (expense claims, advances, an approval workflow, posted to the ledger) and `payroll_erp` (accounts, accrual journal, bank entry). Roles: HR Manager, HR User, Employee, Leave Approver, Attendance Device, Payroll Manager, Expense Approver; field groups `bank`, `identity`, `review` (`packages/hr/entities/people.yaml:99-107`, `packages/hr/entities/talent.yaml:153-188`).
- **People and lifecycle.** Employee with reporting line, approver defaults, holiday list and shift; onboarding and separation checklists whose completion sets the status; promotion and transfer that apply their change on submit (`packages/hr/entities/people.yaml:56-229`, `src/people.rs`).
- **Time.** Holiday lists, shift types (grace, half-day and absent thresholds, overnight), assignments with an overlap check (`src/attendance.rs:450`), check-ins from devices or the app, attendance marked per shift day in the tenant's time zone with late-entry and early-exit flags (`packages/hr/entities/attendance.yaml`, `src/attendance.rs:1-4`).
- **Leave.** Types, policies, allocations; applications through the `Leave Approval` workflow with balance, overlap, holiday, applicable-after and longest-run checks (`src/leave.rs:490-541`); an append-only ledger with carry-forward, encashment and expiry posted as entries (`src/leave.rs:1-4`).
- **Hiring and performance.** Openings, applicants, interviews with feedback ratings, offers that create the employee, appraisal cycles, templates and weighted goals scored by employee and manager (`packages/hr/entities/talent.yaml`, `src/talent.rs`). Since 0.3.4 (`bfac32b`): training programs, sessions that record who passed and until when, per-person records (`talent.yaml:190-244`); they are in no module's `items`, so the sidebar omits them (`packages/hr/package.yaml:31-35`).
- **Payroll engine.** Components with CEL formulas over exact decimals, structures and assignments, progressive tax slabs with declared exemptions, slips prorated by payment days, additional salary, leave encashment, runs that post one balanced accrual and a bank entry (`src/payroll/`). It is a generic engine, not statutory coverage: the registry marks payroll by jurisdiction partial, with no bank-payment file and no independent fixtures (`ferrum/docs/registry/apps.yaml:26`, `ferrum/docs/registry/blockers.yaml:12`). The India-flavoured seed rows and labels are examples (`packages/payroll/package.yaml` seed; `packages/hr/entities/people.yaml:102-104`).
- **Interface.** `ui/src/Home.tsx`: check in and out, leave balances, own requests, leave approvals in place, who is away, new joiners, anniversaries, directory search; everything else through the records frame (`ui/src/main.tsx`). `app.yaml:17-39` adds notification kinds and a Create entry, no `shell.work`, `shell.search` or widget, and `src/lib.rs` registers no site, so there are no custom endpoints. Route lists: FHR 36 (requests, dashboards, slips, check-ins), Ferrum 7 (`referance/_inventory/hr__frappe-hrms.txt`, `referance/_inventory/ferrum__hr.txt`).
- **Reports** (11, `src/reports.rs`): directory, headcount, birthdays and anniversaries, monthly attendance, leave balance, expense claims, recruitment pipeline, salary register, payroll summary, bank remittance, income tax deductions.
- **Inherited frame.** Board by any select such as applicant status, calendar by one date such as an interview, timeline with one bar per record such as leave or a shift (`web/src/app/Views.tsx`); chips, per-user views, bulk bar and peek panel (`web/src/app/ListView.tsx`, `web/src/app/list/`); Discussion and Activity (`web/src/app/Collab.tsx`, `web/src/app/FormView.tsx`).

## Screens to hand-craft

### 1. Requests hub (new page in `ui/src`, plus a `shell.work` endpoint)
- **Purpose and layout.** Employees follow what they asked for; approvers clear their queue with the facts in front of them. A segmented control: Waiting for me (n), My requests, Team calendar. Cards, oldest first: avatar, name, type chip (Leave, Expense, Advance, Attendance, Shift), one-line summary ("Casual Leave, 12-14 Mar, 3 days"), a context line, Approve and Reject (reason popover). Team calendar: a month grid of the approver's reports with leave bars and shaded holidays, so overlaps show before deciding. A click opens the record in a side sheet.
- **Context line.** Leave: balance after this request and "2 of 9 in Support are off on these days". Expense: claimed against sanctioned per line (the approver edits sanctioned inline) and the advance set off. Attendance: that day's punches.
- **Interactions.** A, R, J, K keys; select several and approve; actions call the workflow endpoint as `ui/src/Home.tsx:146-150` does; a card leaves on success and returns on failure; live refresh per entity (`useLiveEntity`). The count feeds the platform's My work through a new endpoint; HR registers no site today, and ferrum-app-helpdesk's `ferrum-app-helpdesk/src/site.rs` (`work`) is the pattern.
- **Data we have.** LeaveApplication (`leave_approver`, status Open, `total_leave_days`, `leave_balance`, workflow `Leave Approval`), approved leave for overlaps, ExpenseClaim (`expense_approver`, workflow `Expense Approval`), EmployeeAdvance. **Missing:** AttendanceRequest and ShiftRequest; a waiting state for expenses, because that workflow starts at Draft (`packages/hr_erp/package.yaml:27-38`) and the approver is told on first save (`src/expense.rs:240-245`), so the hub would list drafts the employee is still writing.

### 2. My attendance and Team today
- **Purpose and layout.** See the month at a glance and fix mistakes before payroll; managers see who is in. A month grid (Monday first), one disc per day: green Present or Work From Home, amber Half Day, red Absent, blue On Leave, grey Holiday or weekly off (name on hover), blank in the future. Under it counts (Present, Half day, Absent, Leave, Late entries, Early exits), total hours and a legend. A Pay tab lists the person's submitted slips (period, net pay) and opens an earnings and deductions view. Manager tab Today: columns In, Not in yet, On leave, Late, each with a count; a name opens the same drawer.
- **Interactions.** Arrows change month; today is ringed; a day opens a drawer: shift, IN and OUT punches with times, working hours, "late by 12 min", linked leave, and Request correction with the date prefilled. Live refresh on `EmployeeCheckin`.
- **Data we have.** Attendance (`status`, `in_time`, `out_time`, `working_hours`, `late_entry`, `early_exit`, `shift`, `leave_application`), EmployeeCheckin, holiday days (the employee's list, else the shift's, else the company's: `people.yaml:86`, `src/calendar.rs`), LeaveApplication, `reports_to` for the team, SalarySlip (its owner can read it). **Missing:** AttendanceRequest; a print format for slips (none in the packages).

### 3. Payroll run review
- **Purpose and layout.** Catch mistakes before a run is submitted and posted. No reference has this comparison table; FHR's `hrms/payroll/doctype/payroll_entry/payroll_entry.js` supplies the attendance check, a failed slip shown with its error and a retry, and releasing withheld salaries. A stepper from `PayrollEntry.status` (Draft, Submitted, Slips Created, Slips Submitted, Paid) over a run header (period, company, filters). A summary strip: employees, gross, deductions, net, each with the change against the previous run. A table, one row per employee: name, department, payment days over working days, unpaid days, gross, deductions, net, change against last run (amount and percent), flag icons; an "Only flagged" toggle. A row expands to earnings and deductions side by side with last run's values, differences marked.
- **Flags** are arithmetic and completeness checks, never a statement that pay is right under any country's law: no active structure assignment, days with no attendance mark (the slip pays them: `src/payroll/slip.rs:1-11`), net at or below zero, payment days below working days, joined or left in the period, a slip that failed to create, change over a threshold, a component added or dropped since last run, bank mode without bank details.
- **Interactions.** Open a slip in a sheet; recalculate one slip; hold or release an employee from the run; "Submit slips" stays disabled until the flagged count is acknowledged, optionally by a second person; after submit the bank step shows remittance totals (`report:bank_remittance`).
- **Data we have.** `PayrollEntry.employees` (`net_pay`, `salary_slip`), SalarySlip (`payment_days`, `leave_without_pay`, `gross_pay`, `total_deduction`, `net_pay`, earning and deduction rows), the previous period's slips per employee, Employee bank fields (group `bank`, readable by Payroll Manager). **Missing:** a hold state (FHR has salary withholding) and a two-person setting.

### 4. Shift roster
- **Purpose and layout.** Plan who works which shift and see cover. Toolbar: month switcher; filters (company required, department, branch, designation); shift-type filter. Grid: a sticky first column of employees (avatar, name, designation); columns are days with weekday letter and date, today highlighted, weekly offs shaded. Cells: shift pills coloured by shift type ("Morning 06:00-14:00"); a holiday cell with its name; an approved-leave cell in blue; an empty cell shows "+" on hover. Footer row: staff per day per shift.
- **Interactions.** Click an empty cell for a popover: shift type, from, to (that day by default), repeat-on-weekday chips, "until further notice". Click a pill to change or delete "this day" or "all consecutive days". Drag a pill to another day or person to move it, swapping when the target holds a shift; refuse overlaps and leave days with the reason. Select several cells to assign at once. Our additions, not in FHR: the footer cover counts, copy last week or month, and a warning when a shift falls below its minimum cover.
- **Data we have.** ShiftAssignment (`employee`, `shift_type`, `start_date`, `end_date`; overlap check `src/attendance.rs:450`; submittable, so a move is cancel and amend), ShiftType times, HolidayList, approved LeaveApplication, Employee filters. **Missing:** shift location, active or inactive assignment, recurring weekday schedule, minimum cover per shift.

### 5. Onboarding and exit board
- **Purpose and layout.** Make sure a joiner or leaver has everything done by the right person. FHR supplies the templates and owned tasks (`hrms/hr/doctype/employee_onboarding_template/`); the board is our layout. Cards per person in progress: name, role, countdown to joining or last day, progress ring (done over total), overdue count. A card opens the checklist grouped by owner (HR, IT, manager, employee, finance): title, assignee, due date, tick, notes. A My tasks tab for assignees. Creating one offers a template chosen by department or designation that seeds tasks with due dates relative to the joining date ("3 days before", "7 days after").
- **Interactions.** Tick in place (the server stamps `completed_on` and sets the status); reassign; add an ad-hoc task; nudge; overdue in red. The platform's My work lists the assignee's open tasks, and a notification tells them when assigned and a day before due.
- **Data we have.** EmployeeOnboarding and EmployeeSeparation with `activities` (`assignee`, `due_date`, `done`, `completed_on`; `src/people.rs:236-267`). **Missing:** a template entity (title, applies to department and designation, rows with owner and day offset), notification kinds for tasks (`app.yaml:35-39` has leave and expense only), a `shell.work` endpoint.

### 6. Organisation chart
- **Purpose and layout.** See who reports to whom and find anyone. Top bar: search and company filter. Canvas of cards (initials or photo, name, designation, department, a badge with the count of direct reports), opened one level at a time from the people with no `reports_to`; a breadcrumb path to the selected card; a side panel with contact details and "Open record"; dashed cards for open vacancies from `JobOpening`.
- **Interactions.** From `hrms/hr/page/organizational_chart/`: click a badge to load that person's reports on demand; search jumps to a person and expands their chain; "Expand all" is a deliberate extra step; zoom and pan; export as an image; a directory list as the alternative view. Our additions: arrow keys between parent, child and sibling, and HR dragging a card onto a manager to change `reports_to`.
- **Data we have.** Employee (`reports_to`, `department`, `designation`, `status`, `company_email`, `cell_number`), JobOpening (`designation`, `department`, `vacancies`, `hired`). **Missing:** a photo field (the platform has an `image` type, `web/src/app/list/RecordPeek.tsx:17`) and a cycle guard: nothing stops A reporting to B and B to A, so draw with a visited set.

## What Ferrum already does better

- **Standalone.** HR and payroll need only the core; ledger posting sits in connectors, so a company can run people without accounting (`packages/hr/package.yaml:5`, `packages/payroll/package.yaml:5`, `CHANGELOG.md` 0.2.0). FHR is installed on the Frappe and ERPNext stack (setup steps in `referance/hr/frappe-hrms/README.md`).
- **Boundaries in the data layer.** Bank, identity and review fields are separate permission groups; employees see only their own records through `employee_user` ownership, enforced by row-level security; reports narrow to the caller's own rows (`packages/hr/entities/people.yaml:99-107`, `packages/hr/entities/talent.yaml:153-188`, `src/reports.rs` header).
- **Privacy and AI.** Payslips, salary assignments, additional salary, applicants, offers, appraisals and separations are `internal` and `restricted`, so the egress rule keeps them out of what goes to an AI provider (commit `60cb184`; `packages/payroll/entities/payroll.yaml:71-72,179-180,282-283`, `packages/hr/entities/talent.yaml:31-32,90-91,168-169`, `packages/hr/entities/people.yaml:160-161`; rule in `ferrum/crates/kernel/src/ctx.rs:331-349`). Personal-data export and erasure state what is kept and why (`packages/hr/package.yaml:52-68`); pending approvals move to the successor when an approver leaves (`packages/hr/package.yaml:193-197`). Not covered yet: Employee's ungrouped personal fields (birth date, addresses, emergency contacts, blood group) pass the rule (`packages/hr/entities/people.yaml:75,90-98`; limit noted in `ferrum/docs/registry/ai.yaml:6`).
- **Exact money.** CEL formulas over exact decimals rounded to six places; an unknown component name is refused at save; progressive tax is projected and spread over the periods left (`src/payroll/formula.rs:1-7`, `src/payroll/slip.rs:1-11`).
- **Leave that adds up.** Every balance change is a ledger entry and cancelling posts reversals; overlap, applicable-after, longest-run, half-day and holiday checks run on the server (`src/leave.rs:1-4,490-541`).
- **Attendance from punches** in the tenant's time zone, with check-in windows, grace periods, half-day and absent thresholds, overnight shifts and a device-sync watermark (`packages/hr/entities/attendance.yaml:28-58`, `src/attendance.rs:1-4`).
- **Training validity** is computed per person and withdrawn when a session is cancelled (`bfac32b`: `src/talent.rs`, `packages/hr/entities/talent.yaml:229-244`).
