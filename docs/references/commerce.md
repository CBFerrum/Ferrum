# Commerce: references

**Compared with:** medusajs/medusa at `7e797dfd591d` (`referance/commerce/medusa`, MIT with an enterprise carve-out) and Ferrum Commerce 0.3.2 at `bdc6efe9afca` (`ferrum-app-commerce`, branch `payments/exceptions`, working tree clean; the tree moved one commit past `823fbb143dd8` while this was written, and the page describes the new state). **Date:** 2026-11-01

Paths: Medusa paths start at `referance/commerce/medusa/packages/`, and `routes/` means `admin/dashboard/src/routes/`. Ferrum paths are inside `ferrum-app-commerce` unless they start with `ferrum/` (the platform at `5ebc51a9960c`; its frame `ferrum/web` is at `311bf43afcef` with a few uncommitted edits, so only committed behaviour is claimed) or `erp-platform/` (the ERP app at `993017fc502a`, clean). `referance/_inventory/` holds no route list for these apps, so absences below come from directory listings and grep.

## What Ferrum has

- **Records (17 entities, `packages/commerce/entities/*.yaml`):** store settings, a category tree, website items (images, SEO, related items), moderated reviews with a verified-purchase flag, carts, coupons, shipping rules, payment requests. Web-order fields sit on ERP's `SalesOrder` through overlays (`packages/commerce/package.yaml`).
- **Selling path:** carts re-priced by ERP's engine on every change (`src/cart.rs`); one idempotent checkout that locks stock per item and makes the order and payment request (`src/checkout.rs`); stock net of open orders (`src/catalog.rs`); unpaid orders expire and release stock (`src/upkeep.rs`).
- **Storefront:** server-rendered pages on a `site:commerce` host with sitemap, robots, schema.org and Open Graph (`src/pages.rs`), cart, checkout and order scripts (`assets/store.js`), a JSON API (`src/site.rs`).
- **Payments:** Stripe, Razorpay and PayPal as WebAssembly plugins over one shared kit; each makes one session per request, verifies signatures, reconciles every five minutes and never reads its own keys, which the host holds (`gateways/*/src/lib.rs`, `credentials` in `gateways/*/manifest.json`). Money a gateway took and could not book becomes a `PaymentException`; a person approves its refund and the plugin sends it (`packages/commerce/entities/payment.yaml`, `src/payment.rs`, `gateways/kit/src/lib.rs`).
- **Admin screens:** one hand-written page, `ui/src/Home.tsx` (sales today, 7 and 30 days; web orders to deliver; reviews moderated in place; failed payments; catalog counts). Every other screen is the shared records frame (`ui/src/main.tsx`). Five reports in `src/reports.rs`.
- **Tracker:** `ferrum/docs/capabilities.md:121` lists refunds under FERRUM-COMMERCE-001 on a keyword probe only; the code refunds whole payments it could not book, and nothing else (row 1).

## Screens to hand-craft

1. **Order desk.**
   - *Purpose and layout:* take a web order from placed to delivered and billed, and answer the customer. Two columns as in `routes/orders/order-detail/order-detail.tsx`: main (lines with stock reserved or not, payment, fulfilment), side (customer, addresses, notes, coupon, timeline). A strip at the top names the next step.
   - *Interactions:* the primary button follows the order's state: create delivery note, mark shipped with tracking number, link and a notify switch (`routes/orders/order-create-shipment`), create invoice. Each payment request is a card with status, expiry countdown, gateway reference, copy link, mark paid for manual and cash on delivery, and refund (`routes/orders/order-detail/components/order-payment-section`). The timeline merges payment events, delivery notes, invoices and audit entries and folds old ones (`order-activity-section/order-timeline.tsx`).
   - *Data:* `SalesOrder` with `commerce_*` fields, its `PaymentRequest` rows (`src/orders.rs`), delivery notes and invoices, the audit endpoint (`ferrum/web/src/app/FormView.tsx`, AuditPanel): all exist. Missing: tracking fields and a refund for a booked payment. Website Manager can only read orders (`packages/commerce/package.yaml`) and the address fields are read-only, so the desk's actions need Sales roles or app actions.
2. **Return and refund.**
   - *Purpose and layout:* a focus dialog from the order (`routes/orders/order-create-return/components/return-create-form`): lines on the left with quantity stepper, reason and note; on the right the returned value, deductions, estimated refund, restock warehouse and a notify switch. Step two receives (expected against counted); step three refunds.
   - *Interactions:* totals move with the steppers; the refund is capped at paid minus refunded and split per payment; a reason is required; it ends in a credit note and a return delivery note; the shopper sees the status on the order page.
   - *Data:* delivered quantities, return delivery notes and credit notes, `PaymentEntry` and `PaymentRequest.gateway_reference` exist; a return-reason master and a refund of a booked payment (the plugins only refund exceptions, in full) do not.
3. **Catalog editor.**
   - *Purpose and layout:* edit a store item on one page: main column (title, address preview, description, media, variants once `Item` has them), side column (published switch, rank, categories, related items, weight, limits, SEO).
   - *Interactions:* drag images and pick the main one (`routes/products/product-media/components/edit-product-media-form`); address made from the title with a live uniqueness check; "open in store" to the item's page (`src/pages.rs`); price and availability shown read-only so staff see what shoppers see; variant prices as a spreadsheet grid with keyboard moves, paste and fill (`admin/dashboard/src/components/data-grid`); a Details, Organize, Variants, Inventory progress bar for a new item (`routes/products/product-create/components/product-create-form`).
   - *Data:* `WebsiteItem` fields exist; price and availability are computed for the storefront (`src/catalog.rs`) but not offered to staff; variants do not exist.
4. **Promotion builder.**
   - *Purpose and layout:* make a discount without knowing `PricingRule`. Pick a template card (amount off products, percent off products, percent off order, free shipping, buy X get Y; `templates.ts`), then one form: code or automatic, value, targets, conditions (minimum, customer group, dates), limits. A sentence beside it restates the offer.
   - *Interactions:* the template hides fields that do not apply; the sentence updates as you type; a "try on a cart" box says whether the offer applies and why not; draft and active status; usage and revenue from the `coupon_usage` report on the detail page.
   - *Data:* `CouponCode`, `PricingRule`, the usage count and the report exist; order, shipping and buy-get targets need engine support first.
5. **Shipping setup.**
   - *Purpose and layout:* say where the store ships and what it charges (`routes/locations`): countries on the left, the rules for the selected country on the right, bands as an editable grid.
   - *Interactions:* overlap and open-band errors shown inline as the server finds them (`src/shipping.rs`, `band_errors`); a destination tester takes country, subtotal and weight and lists the rules that apply with their charge.
   - *Data:* `ShippingRule`, bands and countries exist; the tester can call the same `charge` function behind a read endpoint (not built).

## What Ferrum already does better

- A storefront in the box: server-rendered pages with sitemap, schema.org and Open Graph (`src/pages.rs`); Medusa's packages hold a store API but no storefront pages (`medusa/src/api/store`).
- The books: a web order is an ERP sales order, payments become advances applied at invoicing, taxes come from ERP's templates, so cart, order and invoice agree (`src/cart.rs`, `src/payment.rs`); `packages/modules` has no ledger or invoice module.
- Checkout under per-item stock locks with an idempotency key and a total check (`src/checkout.rs`); open orders reserve stock and unpaid ones release it (`src/upkeep.rs`).
- Abandoned-cart, conversion-funnel and coupon-usage reports with an abandoned-cart event (`src/reports.rs`, `src/upkeep.rs:146`); grep finds no abandoned-cart or funnel code in Medusa's packages. Reviews with moderation and verified purchase (`packages/commerce/entities/catalog.yaml`); Medusa has no review model under `modules/*/src/models`.
- A working admin home with sales and work queues (`ui/src/Home.tsx`); Medusa's home only redirects to orders (`routes/home/home.tsx`).
- Gateway plugins never read their keys (the host signs calls and checks webhooks, bound to the provider's host), settle once, reconcile on a schedule, turn money they cannot book into a record a person decides on, and are tested against provider mocks (`credentials` in `gateways/*/manifest.json`, `src/payment.rs`); Medusa's Stripe provider keeps its key and webhook secret as in-process options (`modules/providers/payment-stripe/src/core/stripe-base.ts`).
- Lists: saved views, column chooser, bulk edit, filter chips, a record peek, board, calendar and timeline come with the frame (`ferrum/web/src/app/ListView.tsx`, `list/SavedViews.tsx`, `list/BulkBar.tsx`, `list/FilterBar.tsx`, `list/RecordPeek.tsx`, `Views.tsx`); Medusa has saved views (`admin/dashboard/src/components/table/save-view-dialog`), the rest not verified.
