# Campaigns: references

**Compared with:** knadh/listmonk `86ef53b46b06`, mautic/mautic `ee2b5b60dd24` against ferrum-app-campaigns 0.1.1 (working tree, `fdd4eb57ab05`) and the records frame in ferrum (`81b7f7c8cd38`, `web/src/app`). **Date:** 2026-11-01

## What Ferrum has

- Engine: 14 entities, 2 roles, 5 reports (`packages/campaigns/entities/*.yaml`, `package.yaml`, `src/reports.rs`). Contacts carry consent evidence (time, source, keyed IP hash) and attributes; one suppression list; segments on fields, attributes, email groups and engagement compile to parameterized SQL (`src/segment.rs`); imports from email groups, customers and CRM contacts and leads (`src/import.rs`); double opt-in signup forms (`src/public.rs`).
- Sending: a campaign is a document and Submit schedules it; each recipient is claimed before the email goes to the Mail app, under one hourly budget shared with newsletters and journeys; A/B tests pick a winner after N hours; signed open, click and unsubscribe links; automatic opens counted apart; a signed bounce webhook (`src/campaign.rs`, `src/send.rs`, `src/delivery.rs`). Marketing User drafts, only Marketing Manager schedules (`package.yaml`).
- Journeys: triggers (group, segment, attribute change, date anniversary), Send, Wait in local time, Branch, Update attribute, group steps, Exit, goal segment; steps are frozen once active (`journey.yaml`, `src/journey.rs`).
- Interface: none of its own (`app.yaml` has no `ui`; FERRUM-UX-004 in `ferrum/docs/registry/backlog.md`). Records use the shared frame: lists with saved views, filters, bulk actions and CSV export; a board by status (cards cannot be dragged, status is read-only); a calendar by schedule date; child-table grids; actions as buttons; comments, assignments, tags (`ListView.tsx`, `Views.tsx`, `FormView.tsx`, `Collab.tsx`, `list/`).
- What the frame cannot do for this app: show an action's result other than a file or a record (`FormView.tsx` `afterAction`, so Preview shows nothing); draw charts (`ReportView.tsx` renders tables); edit HTML (field types stop at text, markdown, code in `ferrum/crates/meta/src/types.rs`); import a file (the `_import` API exists, `ferrum/docs/api.md`, but `ListView.tsx` has no button); duplicate a record.
- Social: nothing. FERRUM-ECOSYSTEM-005 is missing (`ferrum/docs/registry/ecosystem.yaml`); the connector layer it depends on is built but tested only against a stand-in provider (FERRUM-CONNECT-001, `ferrum/docs/registry/extensions.yaml`).

## What the references do that Ferrum does not

Later sections shorten reference paths to file names. listmonk has no `frontend/` at this commit: its admin is rendered by the server from `static/admin/` (Alpine, Chart.js), so its screens are read from those templates and scripts. ## Screens to hand-craft

1. **Email editor and review.** Purpose: write, preview and test an email without touching HTML; today `content` is a textarea and Preview shows nothing.
   - Layout: the campaign page as tabs Setup (name, From account, subject, preheader, audience), Content, Review, Results. Content: block canvas in the middle (text, image, button, divider, columns, footer), block settings on the right, a merge-tag menu (first name, last name, email, attributes, unsubscribe link) in the toolbar, a format switch Design, HTML, Plain text, and a gallery of `EmailTemplate`s. Top bar: Preview (desktop and phone), Send test, Save.
   - Seen in the reference: listmonk's campaign page has tabs Campaign, Content, Attribs, Attachments, Archive, Preview on F9, Save on Ctrl or Cmd+S and a test popover taking several addresses (`views/campaign.html`); its format menu offers rich text, HTML, Markdown, plain and visual, and the visual editor keeps its JSON source beside the compiled HTML (`visual-editor.js`, `content.js`). Mautic's builder is GrapesJS with MJML and 41 starter theme folders (`plugins/GrapesJsBuilderBundle`, `themes/`).
   - Proposed: preview as a chosen contact and variant (the `preview` action takes both); a subject field with a character count and an inbox-row preview (sender, subject, preheader); images from Drive through the `pick file` intent; switching to raw HTML asks first because the design source is dropped; A/B variants as tabs A to E; Review lists links with tracking state, images without alt text, size, a missing postal address and whether the unsubscribe footer will be added.
   - Data: has `content`, `text_content`, `variants`, `preview`, `send_test` (`campaign.yaml`, `src/campaign.rs`), `EmailTemplate`, Drive's pick intent (`ferrum-app-drive/app.yaml`). Lacks a `design` JSON field compiled into `content` on save, a screen for the preview result, and public image URLs for mail clients (how Drive serves public files: not verified).
2. **Campaign results.** Purpose: one place to judge a send, in place of five tables.
   - Layout: header (name, status chip, sent time). KPI strip: Sent, Delivered, Opened by people (automatic opens in a lighter number beside it), Clicked, click rate, click-to-open, Unsubscribed, Bounced (hard, soft), Complaints, each with count and rate (`campaign_performance` returns them). Then a line chart of clicks and first opens per hour for two days and per day after; a link table (URL, unique and total clicks, share of recipients); an A/B card (variant bars, winner badge, countdown to the choice); the recipients list (an embedded `ListView` of `CampaignRecipient` filtered to this campaign).
   - Seen in the reference: listmonk's analytics page takes one or more campaigns, a date range and an interval (hour, day, week, month), draws views, clicks and bounces as lines with a donut per campaign and a link bar chart, and shows unique counts only when individual tracking is on (`views/analytics.html`, `assets/js/views/analytics.js`); Mautic has sent, read and click graphs, a clicks tab of sortable links and a contacts tab (`Email/details.html.twig`).
   - Proposed: compare with up to four other campaigns on one chart; a click on a link row filters recipients to those who clicked it; refresh every minute while Sending, with "updated at", because sending advances once per maintenance round.
   - Data: has per-recipient timestamps and counters, `campaigns_click` (a row per click with time and link number), `campaigns_link`, reports `campaign_performance`, `ab_test_results`, `link_clicks`; recharts is in the platform web (`ferrum/web/package.json`, used under `src/app/studio`). Lacks open events over time (only first open, last open and a count are kept: the chart uses first opens, or add a small open log) and a counts-by-bucket endpoint.
3. **Import wizard.** Purpose: bring an existing list in with the right consent record; only internal sources import today.
   - Layout: five steps in a left rail: Source (file, pasted list, current sources), Map columns, Consent, Check, Run. Map: sample rows left, target right (email, first name, last name, attribute key; a new key must be lowercase letters, digits, underscore). Consent: already consented (a free-text basis is required), needs email confirmation, or opt-out list. Check: counts of new, known, invalid, duplicated in the file and suppressed, with the first 20 problem rows. Run: progress bar, live log, Stop, download of rejected rows.
   - Seen in the reference: listmonk's import has modes subscribe and blocklist, a status for new subscriptions, switches to overwrite names and subscription status, a delimiter, CSV or ZIP, then a progress bar and a log you can stop (`views/import.html`, `assets/js/views/import.js`); Mautic uploads, maps fields, then shows progress (`Import/_upload_form.html.twig`, `_mapping_form.html.twig`, `progress.html.twig`).
   - Proposed: existing contacts are not overwritten unless chosen; the basis text becomes `consent_source` ("import: name, basis"); chunks of 5000 rows; the wizard ends on the contact list filtered to this import.
   - Data: has `POST /api/v1/MarketingContact/_import` (up to 5000 rows, per-row errors; `ferrum/docs/api.md`), consent rules (`src/contact.rs`), suppression, email groups. Lacks a dry run, an import record for a file source (extend `AudienceImport`) and file parsing in the browser.
4. **Journey canvas.** Purpose: draw and watch a journey instead of editing numbered rows.
   - Layout: a full-width canvas under the journey header; palette on the left (Send email, Wait, Branch, Update attribute, Add to group, Remove from group, Exit); the trigger on top with the goal as a pill; cards read "Send: subject", "Wait 2 days", "If opened last email" with Yes and No outputs; the selected card opens a right panel with the fields of a `JourneyStep` row; a problems list in the footer; zoom, pan, Auto-arrange.

5. **Social: accounts, planner, approvals.** Purpose: publish and listen on social networks from Campaigns with the same files and review as email.

6. **Segment builder.** Purpose: define who is in a segment and see it as you type.
   - Layout: left, a Match All or Any switch and rule rows (kind: Contact field, Attribute, Email group, Engagement; field or key; operator; value), the kinds and operators of `SegmentRule`; right, "Who matches": matching count, mailable count (subscribed and not suppressed), a 20-contact sample with consent chips and the reason a contact is not mailable; a "Used by" list of campaigns and journeys at the foot.

## What Ferrum already does better

- Segments cannot inject SQL: rules compile from fixed tables of columns and operators with bound values (`src/segment.rs`); listmonk asks administrators to type SQL conditions (`static/admin/views/subscribers.html`).
- Never twice: each recipient is claimed before its email is queued, under a per-workspace budget lock (`src/send.rs`, `src/campaign.rs`); Marketing Users draft and only Managers schedule (`package.yaml`).
- Consent is evidence: time, source and a keyed hash of the network address; only the person can re-subscribe after an unsubscribe or complaint; the suppression list is re-checked at hand-over (`src/contact.rs`, `packages/campaigns/README.md`).
- Honest numbers: automatic opens (privacy proxies, scanners) are counted apart (`src/public.rs` `machine_open`); no equivalent turned up in listmonk's view tracking (`cmd/public.go`) or Mautic's `EmailBundle`. A/B tests have a fixed split and a timed winner; listmonk has none (searched `cmd/`, `queries/`, `static/admin/`), Mautic has weights, a delay and winner criteria (`app/bundles/EmailBundle/Resources/views/Email/absettings.html.twig`).
- Journeys wait in local time across daylight saving, have goals, re-entry rules and anniversary triggers, and never run a step twice (row lock, `src/journey.rs`).
- One platform: permissions with row-level security, audit trail, personal-data erasure that keeps the suppression entry, MCP access for agents, and the Mail app's accounts, retries and limits (`package.yaml`, `src/send.rs`). Both references bring their own users, SMTP settings and storage.
