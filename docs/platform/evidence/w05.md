`deployments` goes from a commit through the forge, a real container build, someone else's approval and a compare-and-swap
to `ferrum-serve`, a separate process, answering HTTP. Services in `run` are real containers, and one is killed to show it
comes back.

## Found by these tests

- A release could name any `upstream`, so a route could be pointed at an address on our side of the network, and a job at
  one from the process holding credentials. Nothing had noticed because nothing tried. There is now a runtime for what
  runs here, and a resolver for what a customer names.
- An expired preview dropped its routes and kept its resources.
- A build that used up its attempts during an outage stayed `queued` for ever, and its commit could not be built again.
