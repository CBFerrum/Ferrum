Operations connects Ferrum to physical work without pretending to be a controller. It holds the
sites, devices and their identity, the durable ledger of commands sent to equipment, who may dispatch
for each domain, and telemetry apart from business records. The site runtime (`ferrum-site`) keeps
working next to the equipment when the link is down.

It coordinates equipment. It does not control it: robot motion, machine sequencing and every safety
function stay with their own controllers, and nothing here is a functional safety claim.
